# 0015: Desktop chat with a Team panel

Status: accepted

## Choice

Use a sidebar of projects and their chats, one centred conversation, a rounded composer and a side panel. For two to five agents, add Team beside Diff, Terminal and Files. Team holds roles, current work, named waits, side jobs and tasks. The same conversation and composer serve one-agent chats. DESIGN.md and designs/system.html record the selected system. The polished pages are listed in designs/README.md.

## Why

The conversation stays readable while coordination remains available beside it. New and Search share outline icons. User messages fit their text; agent replies are plain text. Both themes use neutral surfaces and one restrained accent.

## Alternatives

A live agent strip crowded the composer. Pinned working turns reduced conversation space. Earlier overview and room directions were rejected in favour of one desktop chat shell. The Team-panel direction was selected as v10 and subsequently refined without changing its layout. Earlier directions remain in git history.
