# Use the window

Part of [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md)'s Run, use and check it; the other parts are listed there.

- **The window's shell** ([0070](decisions/0070-the-windows-shell.md)): the rail at the left has Home at the top (where you are; not a button) and, at its foot, Update while a release is ready above the Settings cog. The top line above the frame has the open chat's title and its More, then at the right Summary's faces and the panel's button, open or closed. The frame holds the sidebar, the chat and the panel; the panel's tabs are a row of their own at its top. Windows' own title bar is still above the top line.
- **Chats and projects in the window:** New (or a project's + in the sidebar) opens a draft, and the chat is made at its first send, in the project picked above the composer. New project, last in that picker, takes a name, a git folder and up to 5 agents with their roles. A project's More in the sidebar has Project settings, which opens Settings > Projects at it: General (name, folder, team) and Checks & merge (its checks read only, the CI choice and merge method) save with one Save. Checks are set only by an agent's tool, so to see some on test data, insert rows into `project_checks` with the service stopped. A chat with more than one member shows every agent's turns in the one timeline (0037: a turn is one block, its work folded into the row above its message) and Summary in the top line (0038): the button is the initials of whoever is working, with a dot when someone needs you, and it opens the Supervisor's current round as Plan (the tasks of its newest hand-off, by `given_in`) over one line for each agent. A step, an agent's line, a row of the status line's list or an agent's initial in the timeline opens that agent as a tab in the workspace: its task, its running turn's row, its earlier blocks and its tasks, with Message to send to it. The message box has no picker (0028): its hint names who gets a message that names nobody, typing @ lists the chat's agents (in a chat with no project, every agent, those not in it under Bring in), only a name picked from that list counts and is blue (a typed `@Ann` is plain text), and "To Ann" or "Bo joins" by Send says who gets what is typed; to see a round without a login, give tasks through the tools with the replay engine held, as in Tasks and waits ([api-tasks.md](api-tasks.md)). The replay engine plays its recording once per process and Gathr reuses an engine for an agent's next turn, so for several turns repeat the recording's rows once per turn.
- **A chat's dot in the sidebar** (0040): one dot at the end of a chat's title, grey and breathing while a turn of it runs, blue once it needs you (an approval, a question, a plan for you, a sign-in, or a chat Gathr told you isn't moving, until it moves or you write in it: [0052](decisions/0052-a-quiet-chat-is-noticed.md)); blue wins, and reduced motion keeps it still. To see both without a login, start the service on the replay engine ([running.md](running.md)): a recording with a `sleep` row holds a turn open, and `approval.jsonl` asks for an approval. `gathr diag health` lists the same chats under `chats`.
- **Archive, Delete and stale in the window:** the top line's More menu and each sidebar row's (it shows while the pointer or the keyboard is on the row) have Archive (Restore once archived) and Delete, each asked first. Archived chats are in the folded Archived group at the foot of the sidebar and open read only, with Restore. The Tasks list groups stale ones under Stale; an agent's stale task opens in its chat's Tasks tab, with Cancel or Restart as its state allows. To see stale tasks on test data, start the service with `GATHR_STALE_DAY_MS=15000` and `GATHR_WAIT_EVERY_MS=2000`: the seed's open tasks go stale in about a minute. Settings > General has the two day counts.

- **Delete a project or an agent** ([0069](decisions/0069-deleting-a-project-or-an-agent.md)): a project's More in the sidebar has Delete after Project settings, and Settings > Projects and Settings > Agents have Delete at the foot of the form. Each opens the one confirm, "Delete Larder?", with what the service counts under it, read when it opens: "4 chats will be deleted. Files are kept.", "2 tasks will be cancelled.", "1 task will be cancelled. Ann becomes lead.", "1 project and 2 chats will be deleted. Files are kept." Delete waits for those words; counts that fail to load show Counts failed with its reference, and Delete stays off. The lists change at once, and an open chat deleted with them gives way to its neighbour in the sidebar, else New, never to a chat going with it. Folders the delete couldn't remove yet show as Some files weren't deleted, with their reference, and the sweep tries them again. A removed agent's old messages keep its name; it is in no list, @ list or team. No Undo. To try one, start a dev service with `--seed`, add a project and an agent on a throwaway git folder through the API, and delete them there.
- **Search in the window:** Search under New, or Ctrl K (Cmd K on macOS), searches chat titles and saved messages across chats (0035); the query stays between openings. Up and Down move one cursor over Chats then Messages, Enter opens the row. A message hit opens its chat centred on that message (a turn's row opens for words it wrote mid-work), tinted briefly and focused, and the view holds there until the person scrolls back to the bottom or sends: `gathr.ui.search.open` times it, `gathr.ui.search.error` records a failed search with its `ref` or a hit no longer in its chat. Seeded text repeats about 40 words, so every long message matches most queries: to try a hit far up a chat, add a distinctive word to one message in a stopped test folder's database (the index follows an `UPDATE` of `body`).
- **The conversation map in the window** (0039): the rail of ticks on a chat's left edge, from three ticks up and only in a pane with 44px left of the column (open the workspace at 1440px wide, or narrow the window, and it goes). The rail is at most half the pane's height and 616px, centred in the pane below its top, so it holds 71 ticks in a 900px window and 59 at 760px. A long tick is a message from the person, a short one the lead's reply to them once its turn has ended; hover one, or reach it with Tab from the composer, for its card (Escape closes it), click it or press Enter to open its message at the top of the view, and Up, Down, Home and End move along the rail. The current tick follows the middle of the view as the chat scrolls, and at the chat's end is the latest message on screen. Earlier days fold into one thick tick each once ticks would sit closer than 6px. `--seed`'s chats are each on one day, and "Seed: 500 messages" has no turn rows, which a real chat always has, so its replies get no ticks and its 177 messages fold as Earlier; "Seed: team of five" shows both kinds. To see days fold, a chat's messages must span days: this slice's screenshots moved the times in the service's answer on its way to the page (`messages.ts`, in microseconds, holds them; changing it in a stopped test folder's database was not tried). `gathr.ui.map.open` times a tick's jump, with how far from the end it was and how many older pages it drew; `gathr.ui.map.error` records a message that could not be drawn.
- **A chat's messages in the window** ([0072](decisions/0072-the-chat-reads-like-a-team-chat.md)): every message is a bubble beside its author's face, yours tinted blue, under one name line (the name, Supervisor, the time, the task and its tick). Point at a message, or Tab into it, and its turn's steps and time show at the end of its name line ("· 6 steps · 13s · 1 failed ›"); a failed step or an agent at work keeps them shown; click them to open the steps under the name line. Messages agents send each other with `message` sit in a thread under the message they belong to, each opening with the receiver's blue @Name or, when something came between, a plain quote of what it answers; Hide folds the thread to its faces and "4 replies". A thumbs-up is 👍 and a count; point at it or focus it for who gave it. How a turn or a task ended is on its message's name line ([0051](decisions/0051-the-giver-hears-when-all-is-in.md)): the task's tick, Cancelled or Failed on its member's last message, "Gathr restarted" or Gathr's reason for a turn it ended, Failed or Not sent, with Continue, Retry or Sign in under the bubble. To see a restart's: send on a dev service with a held replay recording, stop the service while the turn runs, and start it again. To see a team's talk without real engines, start a dev service with a replay engine for each agent and `GATHR_REPLAY_HOLD` and `GATHR_REPLAY_ENV` for the engines' tokens, and call `message` and `react` with an agent's token while its turn is held, as `tests/messages.rs` does.
- **Pause and stop in the window:** the More menu by the chat's title in the top line has Pause, then Resume, or Restart after a stop; the composer's Stop while a turn runs is Stop now for the chat. Pause or resume at the foot of Summary pauses or resumes one agent alone, and the tray has Pause all and Resume all. While paused or stopped, a quiet line after the latest message says so. The replay engine takes an interrupt only while it streams, so to try Stop now on a held round, let it stream first.
- **The person's questions in the window:** Tasks under New lists every open question across chats (`--seed` adds six; seed a folder several times for dozens). Picking one opens its chat's workspace (the panel's button in the top line) at that question in the Tasks tab. The tab lists the chat's tasks in three groups, For you, Open and Closed, each a row with a plain face, its what and one line "#24 · Bo · Blocked"; a row opens its task, Back (Tasks, at the top) returns to the list. A question opens as itself: the asker's message with the question in its bubble, answered there, each question by itself. In the chat it is in the same place, the asker's bubble (Gathr's own message for Gathr's question), with the same block and the same draft: a pick or a note made in one shows in the other ([0073](decisions/0073-gathr-speaks-as-a-speaker.md)). Gathr's own question has no field, so a pick answers it at once; an agent's has one, Notes (Answer when it has no choices), so a pick marks the choice and Send (or Enter) sends it. A refused Send stays in the question: "Not sent", why, and its reference, which opens it in Diagnostics, with what was typed kept. A question's file opens as a tab beside Tasks. The first choice of an agent's choice question reads "<agent>’s pick". Right under the question of one an agent gave, the Discuss switch swaps the choices and notes for its own field, Message to <agent>; each is kept while the other shows. Send then sends the message to the agent alone, the question reads Discussing with your message under it, and in the chat your message is your own, at the time you sent it, as an answer is. An agent's task shows its owner and state on the line under its what, then From (Gathr for a fix it gave after a review) and Why; its foot has only what acts: none when done, Cancel while open, Cancel and Restart once blocked, failed or stopped, Restart once cancelled. The question is back when the agent's turn ends ([0065](decisions/0065-questions-in-the-leads-words.md)); to force a refused Discuss, send one with no message through `POST /api/person-tasks/answer`. To force a refusal, block a window's live socket, cancel the question with `POST /api/tasks/<n>/cancel`, then Send it there.
- **The window's title bar follows Theme** (Settings > General): the page calls the window's `set_theme`, the one command its IPC allows, and only from `http://127.0.0.1` (`crates/gathr-app/capabilities/theme.json`); a refusal is a `gathr.ui.window.theme` error. To check System, drive the window without `WEBVIEW2_ADDITIONAL_BROWSER_ARGUMENTS=--remote-debugging-port`: with it, the page always reads the system as light.
- **Updating Gathr in the window** (0032): Gathr never updates by itself. While a release is ready, a blue Update icon shows on the rail, above the Settings cog; one click updates the service and the window: what runs is stopped and carries on by itself on the new version. Meanwhile a turning loader shows in its place, named "Updating"; the page stays and reconnects, then the window installs its own part of the release and restarts once, and the page loads again. The tray has no update item. Settings > General shows the version, the same Update, Check for updates, and the last failure with its reference. To check: `cargo nextest run -p gathr --test update_room --test app_update`, and in `ui/`, `npx vitest run src/update.test.tsx src/settings/AppVersion.test.tsx`.
- **The window updates itself** (F7): a release build checks `latest.json` at start and every hour, downloads the update and checks its signature, then installs it at once if the window is hidden in the tray, else when it's next hidden. After the person's Update it doesn't wait: when the window signs in to the new service and `<data>/app/current.json` names a release newer than its own, it installs at once, shown or not (`gathr.ui.update.install` with `via: "update"`; `"hide"` otherwise). Only the window restarts, shown or hidden as it was (a marker, `window-restart-shown` or `window-restart-hidden`, in the data folder); the service keeps running. Text typed in a message box and an open dialog don't survive that restart: the page keeps them in memory only. When the service hands over to its own update, the window signs in to the new one by itself (`gathr.ui.window.follow`); when the service stops answering for 3 s while the window is open, the window starts it again the way a launch does, so a new copy that died on trial rolls back at once (`gathr.ui.window.restart`; a failed restart goes to the crash log). A window showing Start failed tries the same every 3 s, so it takes up a folder an old service let go of without a relaunch; to check, hold `<data>/gathrd.lock` from another process while it opens, then release it. To stop a test service for good, quit or stop the window first. A debug build never checks. Events are `gathr.ui.update.check` and `gathr.ui.update.install`. To try one locally: build two versions with `release.mjs` and the Tauri CLI, adding a `--config` whose `plugins.updater` has your own test key's `pubkey`, `endpoints` `["http://127.0.0.1:47199/latest.json"]` and `dangerousInsecureTransportProtocol: true` (sign with that key in `TAURI_SIGNING_PRIVATE_KEY`, never the real one), with `GATHR_APP_FEED=http://127.0.0.1:47199` set so the service checks the same feed; serve the newer one's folder after `release.mjs feeds <version> <folder> http://127.0.0.1:47199` on that port; install the older one with `<setup>.exe /S /D=<folder>` (it also adds a desktop shortcut: delete it, and run `uninstall.exe /S` when done), and open it with `--data`. A PR's build artifact does the same with the real key.
- **What the chat waits on: the status line** ([0050](decisions/0050-what-a-chat-waits-on.md), [0071](decisions/0071-an-agents-state-is-one-mark.md)): one quiet line just above the message box says how the team stands, from each agent's state: "Mira needs you, Ann is working, Bo is blocked", "3 agents are working", "Rev is waiting on PR #18 checks" (what a wait is on is said only when the line waits alone, on one thing). Who needs you is blue, who can't run red. Three dots end it while an agent works, still under reduced motion. Nothing shows when everyone is free, and the line keeps its 28px either way, so nothing above or below it moves. Press it for a row for each agent that isn't free: its face, its name, then its state in the mark's shape and word, what and how long, as its face's card says it ("Working · Show cook times · 40s"); a row opens that agent's tab. A wait whose looks keep failing says so in one word after what it waits on, a shade brighter, in its row and its face's card: "Waiting · PR #18 checks · Can't read · 1h 4m". What no agent is on is Gathr's, said like an agent, last: "Gathr is waiting on PR #18 to land" when every agent is free, "Ann and Gathr are working"; the list has a row for each after the agents', with Gathr's mark: its update ("Working · Update to 0.1.240"), a PR it lands ("Waiting · PR #18 to land · 4m", with "Can't read" when its looks fail) and a command an agent's ended turn left running ("Working · Ann: Run the test suite · 12m"). Gathr's rows open nothing. It follows the service without a reload. A wait's last look and why its looks fail are not in the window: `gathr diag waiting --chat <id> --text` says them, as the `waiting` tool does, and Diagnostics has each failing look's event (`gathr.wait.failing`, `gathr.land.look`). To check it: start a dev service with `--seed`, open a team chat, and compare the line and its list with `gathr diag waiting --chat <id> --text`.
- **A plan in the window** ([0062](decisions/0062-a-plan-is-checked-and-approve-hands-it-out.md)): when the lead writes a plan, its version is the lead's own block in the chat with a chip, "Plan · version 2" and "9 parts", and the checker's thumbs-up under it; text the lead writes after it reads after the chip. Gathr's lines follow it (a check that agrees has none: it is the checker's thumbs-up on the version, [0072](decisions/0072-the-chat-reads-like-a-team-chat.md)): "Plan v1: 3 findings", "Plan v1 came to you before Bo finished checking it.", "Plan v1: Sent back" (opening to your words), "Plan v2: Approved", "Plan v2: Dropped", each with Open plan. The chip and those lines open the plan's tab in the workspace, after Tasks and the agent's, laid out as a task's page: Version 2, the versions as links when there is more than one, the title, who wrote it, when, and the plan's state (Checking, Waiting, Revising, Approved, Dropped, or Replaced on an older version), the plan's text, then Asked, Check ("Bo agrees", "3 findings from Bo", "Bo is checking", "Bo didn't finish"), the check's Findings, the lead's Answers to the version before's findings ("Changed: ..." or "Kept: ..."), the ask's Questions with their answers, Sent back with your words on a version you sent back, and, when Gathr caught a change in a held turn of the ask, Changed: one row for each, "Ann: src/notes.rs, src/tags.rs" (0063). Each part is a row as in Tasks, "Part 2 · Ann · After 1": click one to open its brief in place on a tint (Why, Done when, Proof, Where, Size, After), and the one open closes when you open another. From version 2 a part's line ends in Changed or New against the version before, an open Changed part shows "Was: ..." under each field that differs (an Owner row when its owner changed), and a part that went is an ended row, "Ann · Removed". Once approved, each part is the row of the task it became, and opens that task in Tasks. The tab opens at its top when it shows another version or after your own Send back, Approve or Drop; a check that ends while you read changes the state word and the buttons, not your place. The foot has Drop at the left on the newest version while the plan is open, and, once the version has reached you, Send back and Approve; with nothing to do there is no foot. Send back opens the answer form's field, "What to change", over Cancel and Send back, and sends it to the lead as a turn; Approve makes a task of each part and the lead isn't woken. A refusal shows over the buttons as "Not approved" (or "Not sent back", "Not dropped"), why, and its reference, which opens it in Diagnostics. A plan that has reached you is a line in what the chat waits on ("A plan for you: <title>"), turns the chat's dot blue and is a notification while the window is hidden. It is also in the needs dock above the composer ("Needs you · 1 of 1", "<lead>'s plan: <title> · 9 parts", Open, after any approval waiting), and its tab has the blue dot while it waits on you. To check one without a real lead, start a dev service with `--seed`, a replay engine for each agent and `GATHR_REPLAY_HOLD` and `GATHR_REPLAY_ENV` for the engines' tokens, and call `plan` and `react` with the lead's and the reviewer's tokens as `tests/plan_flow.rs` does; `GATHR_PLAN_CHECK_MS` shortens the 10 minutes after which a check that hasn't ended says "didn't finish".
- **A change Gathr caught in the window** ([0063](decisions/0063-building-waits-for-a-new-asks-plan.md)): in a project's chat with two or more agents, while an ask has no approved plan, a shell command's or Codex's change to an agent's worktree shows when that agent's turn ends as one line in the turn, with a pen-on-file icon: "Mira changed 3 files before the plan was approved". Click or Enter opens it to the files, one per line. The ask's "Plan" line shows with it, and the plan's tab lists it as Changed (20 files, then "and <k> more"). To see one, hold a lead's turn with the replay stand-in, write a file into its worktree from a shell, and release the turn (`docs/running.md`). A turn released by "Just fix it." before it ends adds no line, and neither does a pull, a checkout or a build into an ignored folder. The agent's next turn opens with what it changed (`gathr diag turn <ref> --raw`).
- **A silent turn and a quiet chat** ([0052](decisions/0052-a-quiet-chat-is-noticed.md)): a turn with no progress for 10 minutes (20 while a tool is open) is silent. The status line still says Cass is working, as the turn runs, and Cass's row in its list reads "Working · <what> · silent 12m" until the turn makes progress. Its lead gets one turn saying so. When a chat has work open and nothing moves, the lead is woken, and after 5 minutes of that Gathr says so in the chat, in one line ("Not moving for 6 min: #4 (Cass) and 1 more. Write here to move it on."), and the chat's dot in the sidebar turns blue. Neither happens again for the same thing, also after you write: only something new that stops moving is said, and it is named first. The status line says each agent's state meanwhile. The dot goes when something moves or you write in the chat. To check them, start the service with the times shortened, as `tests/quiet_room.rs` does: `GATHR_STALL_AFTER_MS` for a silent turn, on a recording that holds a turn open, and `GATHR_QUIET_TELL_MS` with `GATHR_WAIT_EVERY_MS` for a quiet chat ([api-tasks.md](api-tasks.md)).
- **Notifications** ([0058](decisions/0058-gathr-tells-you-outside-the-window.md)): when a chat starts needing you, or something new waits on you in a chat that already does, and the window is hidden, minimized or not focused, the installed app shows a system notification: the chat's title, then what waits ("Made-up Lead needs your approval"). One per chat until the window has been in front, if only for a moment; with it in front, none. During Pause all it holds them, except a sign-in and an approval Gathr itself asks for, and Resume all shows one, "Resumed", "3 chats need you". At a start with chats already waiting on you and the window not in front, one: the chat's, or "Waiting on you". A click brings the window forward on that chat; a click on an old one Gathr no longer keeps brings it forward with no chat. There is no setting: Windows and macOS have their own notification settings. The dev window shows none. Every outcome is a `gathr.ui.notify` event (`shown`, `held`, `skipped`, `clicked`; `gone` when a clicked chat no longer exists); read them with `gathr diag query "SELECT time, status, attrs FROM event_log WHERE name = 'gathr.ui.notify' ORDER BY ts"`. The tests: `cargo nextest run -p gathr --lib -E 'test(notice)'`, `cargo nextest run -p gathr --test chat_state --test waiting`, and in `ui/`, `npx vitest run src/open-chat.test.tsx`. What they can't do is show a notification or click one, so those are checked by hand, on the installed Gathr with its own data folder: on a test folder the app's identifier carries a key, so the notification's name and icon aren't known, and a debug build shows as Windows PowerShell. Don't run them on a PC someone is using without telling them: notifications appear on screen.
  - **The owner's check on Windows,** after an update:
    1. In any chat, ask the assistant for something that needs your approval in that chat's mode. For example, in Ask mode: "Write hello into a file note.txt in your folder".
    2. Before it asks, close the window to the tray.
    3. Expect one notification named Gathr, with the chat's title and "<assistant's name> needs your approval", and no sound.
    4. Click it while it is on screen. Gathr opens on that chat. Approve or deny there, then switch to another window within 3 seconds. Ask again: one notification.
    5. Repeat with the window open and in front: no notification.
    6. In the team chat, while it has a question open for you: open Gathr, look at it, close the window, and have an agent there ask something that needs your approval. Expect one notification. Without opening Gathr, let a second one come up: no second notification. Open Gathr, close it again, and let a third come up: one notification.
    7. Tray > Pause all, close the window, ask again: no notification. Tray > Resume all: one notification, "Resumed", "1 chat needs you".
    8. Tray > Quit with a chat that needs you. Open Gathr from the Start menu and switch to another window within 3 seconds: one notification for that chat, or "Waiting on you" with the count.
    9. In PowerShell: `& "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Gathr\gathr.exe" diag query "SELECT time, attrs FROM event_log WHERE name = 'gathr.ui.notify' ORDER BY ts DESC LIMIT 10" --text`. With no `--data` it reads your own folder, which is the point here.
    10. If no notification came at all: Windows Settings > System > Notifications, and see whether Gathr is listed and on.
  - **The owner's check on macOS,** on `/Applications/Gathr.app`, the same steps with these differences:
    1. Update with the blue Update on the rail.
    2. The first notification may first ask whether Gathr may send notifications: choose Allow. Whether macOS asks isn't verified. If nothing shows, or it was refused: System Settings > Notifications > Gathr, and turn Allow notifications on. Note whether the notification is named Gathr or Terminal.
    3. Steps 1 to 6 as on Windows. Closing the window hides it in the menu bar. Note the name and icon the notification shows, and whether it was silent.
    4. Step 7 with the menu bar icon's Pause all and Resume all.
    5. Step 8: Quit from the menu bar icon's menu (closing the window doesn't quit), then open Gathr from Applications and switch to another app at once.
    6. Step 9 in Terminal: `/Applications/Gathr.app/Contents/MacOS/gathr diag query "SELECT time, attrs FROM event_log WHERE name = 'gathr.ui.notify' ORDER BY ts DESC LIMIT 10" --text`.
  - A step that fails on either is a fix in a follow-up PR, with the event lines from the query.
