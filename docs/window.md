# Use the window

Part of [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md)'s Run, use and check it; the other parts are listed there.

- **The window's shell** ([0070](archive/decisions/0070-the-windows-shell.md)): the rail at the left has Home at the top, Agents, Memory, Plugins, Designs and Scheduled under it (the open one lit) and, at its foot, Update while a release is ready above the Settings cog. The top line above the frame has the open chat's title and its More, then at the right the Agents button, the Needs you button and the chat sidebar's button, open or closed. Agents opens a panel like Needs you's: each agent of the chat with its fill ("96K of 500K", New before its first call), what it is doing (Signed out with Sign in, which opens Settings > Engines; Compact once its bar is blue at 90% (Waiting, then Compacting, until its compaction's turn ends); or nothing), a turning ring after its name while its turn runs or its engine runs anything in the background, Lead only with more than one agent, and the commands its engine still runs in the background with how long each has run (a command in mono, a helper by its words), each opening the chat's Background tab; a blue dot on the button while an agent is nearly full or signed out. Needs you opens the chat's open questions, then the approvals waiting, a row each; a row closes the panel and goes to its card, tinted for a moment, where it is answered; a blue dot on the button while any waits. The replay engine's `background` recording holds one command until its `hold` row is let go. The frame holds the sidebar, the chat and, while it is open, the chat's sidebar with its tabs: Home, always first, with Find file at its top, then Open (the Files, Designs and Changes tiles, Changes with its count of changed files once read) and the board; Files once opened, the tree of the project's folder, Shared, the project's Designs and each agent's folder for this chat, under Filter files; Designs once opened, the project's designs (each folder in its part of Designs that holds an HTML page), newest first, each with the agent whose step last wrote in it when the chat's steps say so, "1 page" or its count of options, and Writing while that agent's turn runs, Pick while a question waits on your pick in it, or a check and the letter picked, or No designs; a design opens in its own tab, its page in the same sealed frame as an HTML file's, with A, B, C for its pages in their files' order when it has more than one, and Writing. A question carries a design when every file it attaches is an HTML page right in that design's folder, none twice, one per choice (a few of its pages is fine; files elsewhere, no choices, or a count that differs make it an ordinary question with files): then A, B, C are its choices, in the order its files were given, and Pick in the bar answers it with the option shown, as the choice does on its card; once answered, Picked and a check on the letter picked. The newest open such question wins, else the newest answered one. A tile or link to a page of a design opens the design's tab at that option, and the tile shows the page small behind its name, drawn only while it is on or near the screen (through the Designs screen's views, so it never lets a page open in a tab go). With the tab at least 700px wide, Side by side in the bar shows the option shown and the next, each in its own frame with its letter, its choice and its own Pick. Comment in the bar (pressed while on) points at the page: the part under the pointer is outlined, and a click on it opens a small note box by it in place of what the page does with the click (Cancel, or Escape; Send, or Enter, once it has words); with Comment off the page takes its clicks as before. The box takes the keys only when the frame had the window's focus, as a real click in it gives; a box the page opens by itself, claiming a click, takes none. Send posts an ordinary message in the chat to the agent whose step last wrote in the design, else to whoever a message that names nobody goes to; its first line is `Design note on option A, <the page's full path>: the button "Import orders" at body > main > div > button` (the part's kind, its words cut to 80 characters with double quotes made single, and the path that finds it, heard from the page only in that form), your words under it. A message that begins with that line shows it as a small block above your words: the pin's number, the design and option, and the part. Each note shows on its page as a numbered pin at the part's top right corner, numbered by the order the notes on that page were made, a check once the turn the note started has ended, and no pin when the part can't be found again (it is looked for by its path and words, then, when it has words, by them anywhere in the page, then at its path with the same kind). Only a design's tab in a chat has Comment: an HTML file's tab and the Designs screen don't, and their pages are served as they are. Each time a step or turn of the chat ends (at most once a second), the Designs tab or design tab shown reads its folders again, and the design's frame loads again when a file right in its folder changed, however it was written, back at the page's top as a fresh load is; who wrote it and Writing count only a step that names a full path: Claude Code's Write and Edit, and Codex's file change by the first file it names; Changes once opened, each agent's changed files under its face, name and branch: in its worktree (with no git checkout, in the folder its engine runs in, as its file steps changed them), then under each other git checkout or folder it wrote in with its file tools, by that folder's name and path; each file folding, with its lines (all open up to 600 changed lines, else all shut), read again at each step or turn that ends, the last read shown at once, or No changes; and each file opened from the tree, or from the chat (an agent's link to a file, a tile that isn't a picture, a file on a question; a path that isn't full is in that agent's folder, and in a Markdown file's own links, beside that file), with Copy: Markdown as a page and CSV or TSV as a table, each with Source for its own text (a table draws its first 2,000 rows), an HTML page as it looks, filling the tab and keeping its own colours, its scripts running in a frame sealed from Gathr and from this PC (it loads only the files in its own folder and below, never one named with a leading dot and only the kinds a page loads, and `https:` addresses), with Source for its own text (a page over 2 MB shows as it looks, without Source and Copy), code as numbered lines coloured by its ending (up to 150,000 characters), other text as plain numbered lines, or a picture; with neither Source nor Copy, a PDF as its pages one under another, each the tab's width, drawn by PDF.js only while in or near sight (PDF.js and its worker load when the first PDF tab opens), and an `.xlsx`, `.xlsm`, `.xls` or `.ods` workbook as the same table, its first sheet first, with a small tab per sheet under the path when it has more than one; a PDF or workbook that can't be read, or is locked with a password, shows Can't show this file. A file opened from the chat opens the sidebar when it is closed. Which tabs are open is kept per chat until the window closes. A file tab scrolled partly out of sight isn't drawn. Words in either box list the files whose name holds them in place of what is under the box (No files found when none), a moment after typing stops; a click, or Enter on the marked one (the arrows move it), opens the file and empties the box, and the tree comes back with its folders as they were.
- **The Designs screen,** from Designs on the rail: every design in the agent folder's Designs, the projects' and Chats, in the frame Agents and Plugins share. Its navigation has All designs, then under Projects each part by its project's name (its folder's when no project has it, as Chats) with its count; one picks a part. The page has Search, which narrows by a design's name, then a small heading per part with its count and the designs as cards, newest first: the design's first page as it looks, small, then its name and "1 page" or its count of options with when it last changed (40m, 2h, Yesterday, 3d, then the date). A card's page is the same sealed frame as a design tab's, takes no clicks, and is drawn only while the card is on the screen or within half a screen of it, so a long list holds about a screenful of live pages. With none, No designs yet. A card opens the design large in the same place: Designs to go back, its name, its part, options and time, A, B, C when it has several pages, its page, and Open in chat when its project has a chat that isn't archived: that opens the project's newest such chat with its sidebar open on the design's tab. The list is read when the screen opens; nothing on it redraws while an agent writes.
- **The Scheduled screen,** from Scheduled on the rail: everything agents scheduled in every chat, oldest first, each row the agent's face and the schedule's short name, a disabled one muted, or Nothing scheduled; in the frame Memory and Plugins share. The open one's page: its name as the title, the agent's name and `in <chat>, <project>` (a click opens the chat), Run now (a run at once; the next time stays) and Delete (asked first), Enabled with `When disabled, it doesn't run.`, When in the service's words (`Weekdays at 9:00`, `Once, 23 Nov at 14:00`), Next (`Tomorrow, 9:00`; none while disabled), Message (its words, whole, keeping their line breaks), then Runs, newest first, the last 20: when it ran (a skipped one, the day it was due), Running, Done, Ran late, Skipped, Failed or Stopped, and one muted line (the agent's answer's first line, `Due at 9:00. Gathr wasn't running or this PC was asleep.`, or the turn's reason); a click opens its chat at the run's message. Nothing there changes its words or time. It redraws on `schedules`, `agents` and `chat`. In a chat, a `schedule` step is a quiet line under the agent's message, `Scheduled weekdays at 9:00: <its name>`, read from the step's answer, whose `schedule <id>` line opens the screen at it. A run's words are drawn as your message, a bubble at the right, with Scheduled's mark and `Scheduled · 9:00 AM` (when it was due, on your clock) in place of its time.
- **The Memory screen,** from Memory on the rail: About you, then under Projects each project and under Agents each agent with its face, then under Tools each tool added in Plugins with the plug Plugins gives it (no group without tools), in the frame Agents and Plugins share. A place's page has its title, a sentence on what it holds, then its notes newest first, each with who added it and when (`Ann · 3d`, `You`, or nothing for a line written in the file by hand) and a More with Edit (the note becomes a box with Cancel and Save) and Remove (asked first); with none, Nothing yet. Under them, Add a note and Add (Enter adds too); a refusal, a repeat or a note over 200 characters, shows under the box. It redraws when an agent keeps a note or the tools change. In a chat, each note an agent kept in a turn is a quiet line under its message, `Noted about you: …` (`Noted about <project>`, `Noted about <tool>`, `Noted`, `Changed note …`), read from the turn's `note` step, so it is there after a reload; a click opens Memory at that place (a tool's by the `place tool:<id>` line of the step's answer), and Back returns to the chat. A tool an agent added (`add_tool`) or a skill it wrote (`add_skill`) is the same kind of line, on one row, `Added tool Notes` or `Added skill branch-names` (`Changed skill …`), and a click opens its page in Plugins; a tool that needs something says so, `· needs key` with Add, `· needs sign-in` with Sign in, or `· needs NOTES_KEY` (`· needs 2 values`) with Add, each opening the tool's page straight into the key dialog, the sign-in or Change; once the tool has it, the need goes and the button reads Added (Signed in), off. To see them without a login, play a recording whose step's result is the tool's real answer (call the tool through `/mcp` with a held engine's token first).
- **Connect,** in Settings under Background (its mark the owner's, in `ConnectMark.tsx`): one line to run in a terminal on this PC, then that AI app can send work to the team and add skills, tools, agents and projects with no approval in Gathr. App is a dropdown: Claude Code, Codex, Another app. Claude Code's line is `claude mcp add --transport stdio --scope user gathr -- "<gathr>" connect` and Codex's `codex mcp add gathr -- "<gathr>" connect`, with Copy; Another app has Program and Arguments, each with Copy, for an app that takes a command in its own settings. `<gathr>` is the full path the service gives (`connect` in [api.md](api.md)), with `--data` for a dev service; without one, "Gathr’s command line isn’t on this PC." What an app added says "Added by <app>", muted, in the line under the title of its tool's or skill's page in Plugins, its agent's page on the Agents screen and its project's page in Settings > Projects. Words an app sent (`send`) are drawn as an agent's main message is (the owner's ask, 2026-11-22): the app's logo where an agent's picture is and its brand as the card's name, "Claude" for an app whose name holds claude, "Codex" for one whose name holds codex, one whose name holds chatgpt or openai its own name with OpenAI's logo, any other app its own name with Connect's mark (`EngineLogo.tsx`, the logos assistant-ui's, unchanged); a reply from an app in a thread the same, at a reply's size. To see it without a login, start the service on the replay engine and pipe MCP messages into your own build's `gathr --data <folder> connect`.
- **No system title bar** (0070): the top line is the window's bar. Press any part of it that isn't a control to move the window, double-click it to maximize or restore. On Windows, Minimize, Maximize (Restore while maximized) and Close are at its right, Close in the window's top right corner; the edges and corners resize, Win+Arrow snaps. On the Mac the system's three buttons are at its left. Every screen has the top line, a chat or none, and it stays live while a dialog (Settings, New project, a confirm) is open: the dialog holds the same band over it. "Start failed" keeps the system's title bar. With no title bar, a test folder's window name ("Gathr (dev 47101)") shows only in Alt+Tab and the taskbar's preview. The page's commands are `capabilities/window.json`'s; one the window refuses is a `gathr.ui.window.frame` error, named for the command. A test can't drag a native window: `ui/src/TopLine.test.tsx` checks what the page draws and sends, and the rest is checked by hand in the dev window (`gathr dev --window`) or the installed app on its own `--data` folder.
- **Chats and projects in the window:** New (or a project's + in the sidebar) opens a draft, and the chat is made at its first send, in the project picked above the composer and to the agent picked beside it. New project, last in the project picker, takes a name, a git folder and up to 5 agents with their roles. The agent picker, behind the face of who gets the message, lists the assistant or the project's lead first, then the others it can go to by name; it shows only when there is someone else to pick, and not in the assistant's chat. A project's More in the sidebar has Settings, which opens Settings > Projects at it: its name, folder and team, saved with one Save. The message box has no picker of its own (0028): a new chat's hint names who gets a message that names nobody (the agent picked beside the project, else the assistant picked on the Agents screen, else the first agent, or the project's lead; with no agents at all the box is off with No agents yet, and a new chat has Add agent), typing @ lists the chat's agents with their descriptions (in a chat with no project, every agent, those not in it under Bring in; in a new one, the assistant first and nobody under Bring in), a name picked from that list is blue in the box, and once sent a typed `@Ann` that is the exact name of one the list offers counts as picked and is blue in the message too (the box and the sign by Send see only picks), and by Send who gets what is typed: every agent picked, "To Ann" or "To Ann and Bo" with their faces, "Bo joins" or "Bo and Cy join" for agents a chat with no project brings in ([0028](archive/decisions/0028-team-chats.md)). The replay engine plays its recording once per process and Gathr reuses an engine for an agent's next turn, so for several turns repeat the recording's rows once per turn. The top line's More and a chat's row in the sidebar have Rename: "Rename chat", one Name of up to 60 characters, Cancel and Rename; a first message of 40 characters or more gets a name from the background model (Settings > Background) a moment after it is sent, and a name the person gave is never replaced.
- **The chat** ([DESIGN.md](../.impeccable/design-full.md#conversation-and-composer) has its look): the person's messages are bubbles at the right, each agent's main message a card with its picture at the left, and replies fold under the message they answer behind "3 replies · Rev, Ann · 9:21". Under a message, once its turn ends: its time, Copy, and the thumbs-up (Like), filled once yours is given, with who gave one beside its count while the pointer is on it. A card's steps are one line ("Read 4 files, ran 2 commands") that opens them, a failed one with its first line of output; what the card already shows (a kept line, a shown table or chart) isn't in it. A turn that failed says why under its words, with Retry, which sends your message again to the same agent. Once its turn ends, the files its steps changed are a card under its words, and those steps leave the line: "Edited 3 files" with the lines added and removed, View changes (it opens Changes in the chat's sidebar), then a row per file with its own counts, the first three and "Show 2 more files"; a row opens the file. An approval sits at the foot of the card that waits on it, "Run a command?" (or "Edit a file?", "Allow <tool>?") with the command or the file and, when Gathr itself asks, why: a write outside the agent's folders (Claude Code's, asked through Gathr's hook) says what Allow covers, the git checkout the file is in ("Allow covers <checkout> in this chat."), else the files right in its folder ("Allow covers files in <folder> in this chat."), and that agent's later writes there in this chat aren't asked again. A question to you is a card with its choices as rows, Something else… and Answer.
- **The Browser tab** (the chat's right panel, the globe after Changes; Home's Browser tile says how many pages are open): the person's one browser, the same pages in every chat. Each open page is a tab after the fixed ones, as a file is, with its title and close mark; what doesn't fit is in the "+N" list. Under the tabs, Back, Forward, Reload (Stop while loading), the address (a lock for https; empty for a blank page) and New page (a plus): Enter goes there, words that aren't an address search with Bing; with no page open, No pages. New page opens a blank page, shows it and puts the keys in the address. Under it the page live, laid out at the tab's size, so widening the panel widens the page. A click gives it the keys (a quiet ring); mouse, wheel and keys go to the page; Ctrl+C copies the page's selection and Ctrl+V pastes text, on the person's own key press. The pointer over the page is the page's (the hand over a link, the text bar over a field or text, a resize or move pointer where the page sets one, the arrow for a page's own picture pointer and inside a frame from another site), and the arrow when it leaves. A link that opens a new window opens a tab and shows it. A page's own alert, confirm, prompt and question on leaving ("Leave this page?" with Stay and Leave) show as a card over the top of the page, headed "<site> says", answered with OK and Cancel; it never covers the chat or the address row. While it is open the page takes no mouse or keys; Back, Forward, Reload, the address, New page, other pages and closing the page still work (going elsewhere ends an alert, confirm or prompt; a question on leaving asks again for the new move). It takes the keys when they were in the page or the address row (Enter presses the focused button, Escape is Cancel, OK for an alert, Stay on leaving), never from the message box, and gives them back to the page once answered. A dialog in a page not shown waits there until that page is shown. A download or an upload says so in one line over the page's foot ("Downloads aren't built yet", "Uploads aren't built yet"); camera, microphone, location and notifications are refused. Typing with an input method editor (Japanese, Chinese) isn't handled. The tab keeps its page while the panel shows another tab or another chat; the picture stops until it shows again. With no Edge (Windows) or Chrome or Edge (Mac), the tab says what to install. To check it by hand: a dev service, the Browser tab, an address, then the page used as above.
- **The Background tab** (Home's Background tile, after Terminal, says how many run, or None; its mark is two diamonds): what the chat's agents left running in the background, and what ended. Running with its count, each row the agent's face (with more than one agent), what it is (a command in mono), how long it has run and Stop (a filled square, red on hover; the engine's own stop). Finished with its count, folding, newest first, each with how long it took, Failed in red or Stopped, and Clear finished (a bin) at its right. A row whose engine said where its output went opens to the last 64 KB of it ("Output is gone" once the file is). The tab shows the Working ring while anything runs. An agent's card whose turn left something running keeps "Working · 1m" under it, timed from the oldest such command, until all of it ends; the chat's row keeps its ring as well. With nothing at all, No background tasks.
- **The Terminal tab** (Home's Terminal tile, the last tile, says how many of the chat's terminals run, or None, as every tile does when empty): each of the chat's terminals is a tab after the pages, as a file is: "Terminal" for the person's, or an agent's with its face and the command it ran. A tab shows the Working ring while its process runs and the Can't run mark when it ended with a non-zero code; on the shown tab the mark sits where × is and × appears on hover. The tile shows the last one, or starts one when there is none. Under the tabs, the folder (an agent's as "<name> · <folder>"), New (a plus; with more than one folder, a menu of the project's folder, Home for a chat without a project, and each agent's worktree, each by its folder's name with its owner in grey) and Stop (a line square while it runs, red on hover). Under them the terminal at the tab's size, in the window's colours, 10,000 lines kept; an address in it opens in a new page of the Browser tab. Once it ends its output stays, with "Ended" ("Failed, code N" in red when it said so and Stop didn't end it) and Run again at its foot. Closing the tab ends it and forgets it; closing an agent's running terminal first asks at its foot, "Stop <command>? <agent> is using it.", with Keep and Stop. The shell is PowerShell 7 where it's installed, else Windows PowerShell; on a Mac the login shell. Terminals keep running while the window is closed; after Gathr restarts they come back ended, with what they printed. An agent's own terminals start this way too (`terminal_run`); its steps read "Ran in terminal", "Read terminal" and so on with the command, and open that tab (the person's own shows as "yours"). It may ask the person to run a command themselves: a card on its turn, "Run this yourself?" with the command and "In <folder>", and Not now, Open in terminal (a terminal of the person's in that folder, the command typed in, waiting for their Enter, with "Enter to run" at its foot until they type) and Run (the same, run at once). To check it by hand: a dev service, the Terminal tile, a command typed, `exit`, Run again, then the service stopped and started again.
- **A chat's mark in the sidebar** (0040, 0071): one 9px mark after a chat's title, the most pressing of Needs you (a blue dot: an approval, a question or a sign-in), Can't run (a red disc with !) and Working (a blue three-quarter ring, turning; still under reduced motion). To see both without a login, start the service on the replay engine ([running.md](running.md)): a recording with a `sleep` row holds a turn open, and `approval.jsonl` asks for an approval. `gathr diag health` lists the same chats under `chats`.
- **The assistant's chat:** under New and Search, the assistant's row: its face and name, lit while its chat is open, with that chat's mark; no menu. A click opens its one chat, a draft until its first message as New's is, and the chat is in no list of the sidebar. Its top line shows the assistant's face and name, its More has no Rename, no card or panel says Lead, the box names everyone in it ("Message Mira and Kit"), and the chat's sidebar stays closed until opened. An agent joining or leaving is a quiet centred line with its small face, "Kit joined", "Kit left", where it happened (a joined line before the agent's first card); everything said stays. Archive or Delete it as any chat: an archived one sits in Archived as "Assistant", and the row then opens a new draft. To see it without a login, start the service on the replay engine with a recording whose words name another agent with `@Name`, and send `send_out` with the assistant's engine token (`GATHR_REPLAY_ENV`).
- **A project the assistant proposes** (the owner's pick of three, 2026-11-22: "it shows the team first"): under the assistant's words, a framed card with the project's name, its folder under it in mono type, a row per agent (small face, name, Lead on the lead, its description muted), then Make project and Change. Make project makes it; its buttons are off meanwhile, and a failure shows as the corner card New project's Create shows, the buttons left. Change opens New project filled in with the proposal (name, folder, the agents switched on, the lead): Create makes it as the form then says, Cancel leaves the card as it was. Once made, the buttons give way to a quiet line with a folder, "Made project Garden app", that opens the project's first chat, and the rows show the team as it was made; the sidebar shows the project and its chat as they come. A card never used stays as it is. In the project's chat the brief is drawn as the person's message, with the assistant's small face and "From Mira" where its time would be. To see it without a login, send `propose_project` with the assistant's engine token on the replay engine (`GATHR_REPLAY_ENV`), with a git folder.
- **An agent the assistant proposes** (the owner's pick of two, 2026-11-22: "it shows the agent first"): under the assistant's words, a framed card with the agent's face (the one it will have), its name and what it is for muted under it, then Account and Model as the Agents screen names them ("Claude Code · Personal", "Sonnet 5.5"), then Add agent and Change. Add agent adds it as Add on the Agents screen does; its buttons are off meanwhile, and a failure shows as the corner card that screen's Save shows, the buttons left. Change opens the Agents screen's form for a new agent filled in with the proposal (face, name, description, account, model): Save adds it as the form then says and opens its page, and going back leaves the card as it was. Once added, the buttons give way to a quiet line with the Agents mark, "Added agent Kit", that opens its page on the Agents screen, and the card shows the agent as it was added. A card never used stays as it is. To see it without a login, send `propose_agent` with the assistant's engine token on the replay engine (`GATHR_REPLAY_ENV`).
- **A change the assistant proposes to an agent or a project** (the owner's pick of two, 2026-11-24, A: the changes only): the create card's frame with the agent's face, name and description, or the project's name and folder, as they are; then only the fields that change, each its old value struck through, an arrow and the new one (Name, About, Account, Model; Name, Folder), and Look as the old face, an arrow and the new one; when a project's team changes, a Team row and the new team, the lead tagged Lead, an agent who joins tagged Joins, and each who leaves dimmed and struck through, tagged Leaves. Save and Change: Save saves it, its buttons off meanwhile, a failure as the corner card; Change opens the agent's page or the project's settings. Once saved, a quiet line, "Changed agent Scribe" or "Changed project Garden", that opens them.
- **Archive and Delete in the window:** the top line's More menu and each sidebar row's (it shows while the pointer or the keyboard is on the row) have Archive (Restore once archived) and Delete, each asked first. Archived chats are in the folded Archived group at the foot of the sidebar and open read only, with Restore.

- **Delete a project or an agent** ([0069](archive/decisions/0069-deleting-a-project-or-an-agent.md)): a project's More in the sidebar has Delete after Settings, a project's page in Settings > Projects has Delete at its title's right, and an agent's page on the Agents screen has it in its header. Each opens the one confirm, "Delete Larder?", with what the service counts under it, read when it opens: "4 chats will be deleted. Files are kept.", "2 tasks will be cancelled.", "1 task will be cancelled. Ann becomes lead.", "1 project and 2 chats will be deleted. Files are kept." Delete waits for those words; counts that fail to load show Counts failed with its reference, and Delete stays off. The lists change at once, and an open chat deleted with them gives way to its neighbour in the sidebar, else New, never to a chat going with it. Folders the delete couldn't remove yet show as Some files weren't deleted, with their reference, and the sweep tries them again. A removed agent's old messages keep its name; it is in no list, @ list or team. No Undo. To try one, start a dev service with `--seed`, add a project and an agent on a throwaway git folder through the API, and delete them there.
- **Search in the window:** Search under New, or Ctrl K (Cmd K on macOS), searches chat titles and saved messages across chats (0035); the query stays between openings. Up and Down move one cursor over Chats then Messages, Enter opens the row's chat. `gathr.ui.search.error` records a failed search with its `ref`.
- **Pause and stop in the window:** the More menu by the chat's title in the top line has Pause, then Resume, or Restart after a stop; while a turn runs and nothing is typed, the message box's Send is Stop chat, which stops the chat now. A paused or stopped chat says Pausing…, Paused or Stopped above the box, with Resume or Restart. The tray has Pause all and Resume all. The replay engine takes an interrupt only while it streams, so to try Stop chat on a held round, let it stream first.
- **The window's own theme follows Theme** (Settings > General), for what the system still draws of it: the page calls the window's `set_theme`, only from `http://127.0.0.1` (`crates/gathr-app/capabilities/theme.json`); a refusal is a `gathr.ui.window.theme` error. To check System, drive the window without `WEBVIEW2_ADDITIONAL_BROWSER_ARGUMENTS=--remote-debugging-port`: with it, the page always reads the system as light.
- **Updating Gathr in the window** (0032): Gathr never updates by itself. While a release is ready, a blue Update icon shows on the rail, above the Settings cog; one click updates the service and the window: what runs is stopped and carries on by itself on the new version. Meanwhile a turning loader shows in its place, named "Updating"; the page stays and reconnects, then the window installs its own part of the release and restarts once, and the page loads again. The tray has no update item. Settings > General shows the version, the same Update, Check, and the last failure: "Last check failed" on the row, or, while a release is still ready, a card with its reference. To check: `cargo nextest run -p gathr --test update_room --test app_update`, and in `ui/`, `npx vitest run src/update.test.tsx src/settings/AppVersion.test.tsx`.
- **The window updates itself** (F7): a release build checks `latest.json` at start and every hour, downloads the update and checks its signature, then installs it at once if the window is hidden in the tray, else when it's next hidden. After the person's Update it doesn't wait: when the window signs in to the new service and the service runs a release newer than its own, it installs at once, shown or not (`gathr.ui.update.install` with `via: "update"`; `"hide"` otherwise). Only the window restarts, shown or hidden as it was (a marker, `window-restart-shown` or `window-restart-hidden`, in the data folder); the service keeps running. Text typed in a message box and an open dialog don't survive that restart: the page keeps them in memory only. When the service hands over to its own update, the new one takes up the old one's token and the page's sign-in (left in `handover.json`), so the page's requests go on, and the window signs in to it by itself (`gathr.ui.window.follow`); when the service stops answering for 3 s while the window is open, the window starts it again the way a launch does, so a new copy that died on trial rolls back at once (`gathr.ui.window.restart`; a failed restart goes to the crash log). A window showing Start failed tries the same every 3 s, so it takes up a folder an old service let go of without a relaunch; to check, hold `<data>/gathrd.lock` from another process while it opens, then release it. To stop a test service for good, quit or stop the window first. A debug build never checks. Events are `gathr.ui.update.check` and `gathr.ui.update.install`. To try one locally: build two versions with `release.mjs` and the Tauri CLI, adding a `--config` whose `plugins.updater` has your own test key's `pubkey`, `endpoints` `["http://127.0.0.1:47199/latest.json"]` and `dangerousInsecureTransportProtocol: true` (sign with that key in `TAURI_SIGNING_PRIVATE_KEY`, never the real one), with `GATHR_APP_FEED=http://127.0.0.1:47199` set so the service checks the same feed; serve the newer one's folder after `release.mjs feeds <version> <folder> http://127.0.0.1:47199` on that port; install the older one with `<setup>.exe /S /D=<folder>` (it also adds a desktop shortcut: delete it, and run `uninstall.exe /S` when done), and open it with `--data`. A PR's build artifact does the same with the real key.
- **Notifications** ([0058](archive/decisions/0058-gathr-tells-you-outside-the-window.md)): when a chat starts needing you, or its agents have all stopped, and the window is hidden, minimized or not focused, the installed app shows a system notification: the chat's title, then "Needs you" or "Work stopped". Stopped is told at the second read (5 s apart) that finds no turn of the chat running or about to start by itself, whatever stopped it: the work is done, an agent is out of usage, or the chat was paused, by you or at the hand-off limit. Not while it needs you, during Pause all, or once the window has been in front since it stopped. Needs you comes once per chat until the window has been in front, if only for a moment; with it in front, none. During Pause all it holds them, except a sign-in and an approval Gathr itself asks for, and Resume all shows one, "Resumed", "3 chats need you". At a start with chats already waiting on you and the window not in front, one: the chat's, or "Needs you". A click brings the window forward on that chat; a click on an old one Gathr no longer keeps brings it forward with no chat. There is no setting: Windows and macOS have their own notification settings. The dev window shows none. Every outcome is a `gathr.ui.notify` event (`shown`, `held`, `skipped`, `clicked`; `gone` when a clicked chat no longer exists); read them with `gathr diag query "SELECT time, status, attrs FROM event_log WHERE name = 'gathr.ui.notify' ORDER BY ts"`. The tests: `cargo nextest run -p gathr --lib -E 'test(notice)'`, `cargo nextest run -p gathr --test chat_state`, and in `ui/`, `npx vitest run src/open-chat.test.tsx`. What they can't do is show a notification or click one, so those are checked by hand, on the installed Gathr with its own data folder: on a test folder the app's identifier carries a key, so the notification's name and icon aren't known, and a debug build shows as Windows PowerShell. Don't run them on a PC someone is using without telling them: notifications appear on screen.
  - **The owner's check on Windows,** after an update:
    1. In any chat, ask the assistant for something that needs your approval in that chat's mode. For example, in Ask mode: "Write hello into a file note.txt in your folder".
    2. Before it asks, close the window to the tray.
    3. Expect one notification named Gathr, with the chat's title and "Needs you", and no sound.
    4. Click it while it is on screen. Gathr opens on that chat. Approve or deny there, then switch to another window within 3 seconds. Ask again: one notification.
    5. Repeat with the window open and in front: no notification.
    6. In the team chat, while it has a question open for you: open Gathr, look at it, close the window, and have an agent there ask something that needs your approval. Expect one notification. Without opening Gathr, let a second one come up: no second notification. Open Gathr, close it again, and let a third come up: one notification.
    7. Tray > Pause all, close the window, ask again: no notification. Tray > Resume all: one notification, "Resumed", "1 chat needs you".
    8. Tray > Quit with a chat that needs you. Open Gathr from the Start menu and switch to another window within 3 seconds: one notification for that chat, or "Needs you" with the count.
    9. In PowerShell: `& "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Gathr\gathr.exe" diag query "SELECT time, attrs FROM event_log WHERE name = 'gathr.ui.notify' ORDER BY ts DESC LIMIT 10" --text`. With no `--data` it reads your own folder, which is the point here.
    10. If no notification came at all: Windows Settings > System > Notifications, and see whether Gathr is listed and on.
  - **The owner's check on macOS,** on `/Applications/Gathr.app`, the same steps with these differences:
    1. Update with the blue Update on the rail.
    2. The first notification may first ask whether Gathr may send notifications: choose Allow. Whether macOS asks isn't verified. If nothing shows, or it was refused: System Settings > Notifications > Gathr, and turn Allow notifications on. Note whether the notification is named Gathr or Terminal.
    3. Steps 1 to 6 as on Windows. Closing the window hides it in the menu bar. Note the name and icon the notification shows, and whether it was silent.
    4. Step 7 with the menu bar icon's Pause all and Resume all.
    5. Step 8: Quit from the menu bar icon's menu (closing the window doesn't quit), then open Gathr from Applications and switch to another app at once.
    6. Step 9 in Terminal: `/Applications/Gathr.app/Contents/MacOS/gathr diag query "SELECT time, attrs FROM event_log WHERE name = 'gathr.ui.notify' ORDER BY ts DESC LIMIT 10" --text`.
  - A step that fails on either is a fix in a follow-up PR, with the event lines from the query.
